Description

Video to MP3 turns the videos already sitting in your gallery into clean audio files. Open the app, tap MP3 next to any video, drag the two handles to keep the part you want, choose a format and bitrate, and tap Start. The file goes straight into Music/Video to Audio, where your file manager and every other music app can find it. The conversion runs on your device with a built-in MP3 encoder, so there is no upload queue and no account to create.

Everything you need in one app:

  • Extract audio from video in a tap, or several videos in one batch
  • Convert MP4, MOV, 3GP, MKV and WEBM videos to MP3
  • MP3, M4A (AAC), OPUS, WAV and M4R output
  • 64 to 320 kbps — 192 kbps is the free default
  • Trim with a waveform, fade in/out, normalize loudness
  • Set a clip as ringtone, notification or alarm sound
  • Edit title, artist, album and cover art on MP3s
  • Use a video frame as the MP3's cover art
  • Your original video is never changed or deleted
  • Audio tools: cut, join, mix, split, volume boost, recorder
  • Built-in player: queue, shuffle, repeat, speed, equalizer
  • Sleep timer & background play with notification controls

Frequently asked

Does it need an internet connection?

Converting does not. The audio is extracted and encoded on your phone, and your videos are never uploaded. The app does use the internet for ads, purchases, anonymous analytics and crash reports, and for the online song-info lookup if you use it.

Which video formats can I convert?

Most videos saved on your phone, including MP4, MOV, 3GP, MKV and WEBM. The app uses Android's own decoders, so a file your phone cannot open will not convert either.

What audio formats and quality do I get?

MP3, M4A (AAC), OPUS (Android 10 and newer), WAV and M4R, plus FLAC with Premium. MP3 runs from 64 to 320 kbps: up to 192 kbps is free, while 256 and 320 kbps cost 1 credit per file or come with Premium.

Is the app free?

Yes, to download and for the core features: extracting audio, trimming, 64–192 kbps, ringtones, the tag editor and the player. Credits pay for 256/320 kbps, effects such as fades or noise reduction, and every file beyond 3 in one batch. You start with 5 credits and can earn 1 per rewarded ad, up to 5 a day. Premium removes ads and credit costs.

How do I make a ringtone from a video?

Tap MP3 on the video, drag the handles around a 20–30 second clip and tap Start. On the result screen tap Ringtone and choose Phone ringtone, Notification sound or Alarm sound. Android asks once to let the app modify system settings. Step-by-step guide.

Where do my files go?

Into the Music/Video to Audio folder on your internal storage, added to your Music library so other apps see it. The Music tab and Recent files list everything the app has made.

Does converting change or delete my video?

No. The video stays exactly where it was, untouched.

Is there a limit on video length or size?

The app sets no length or size cap and adds no watermark. Long videos simply take longer and need room for the output: one hour of MP3 at 192 kbps is about 86 MB.
