Extract audio from long videos when your phone is low on memory

FightTechSeptember 20, 20262 min read

Long videos often take a long time to process and require additional temporary storage. If your memory is nearly full, just looking at the expected MP3 file size may not be enough. Start with the part you really need and hold back on the source until you've checked the results.

What you will do

Select the segment to listen to, the appropriate bitrate and check the free space before extracting audio from a long video using Video to Audio.

Steps

  1. Check free space in Android Settings before opening a long video.
  2. Select a segment to listen to instead of the entire video if the goal is just one lesson or one answer.
  3. Select the appropriate bitrate for the content; speech does not necessarily need the highest level.
  4. Wait for the save to complete, listen to the test, then decide to delete the test versions you no longer need.

Size estimation is just a preparatory step

With audio compressed at a fixed bitrate, the data portion size is roughly the bitrate multiplied by the duration divided by eight. But the final file also has information attached, and the conversion process can use temporary files. Therefore, you should leave extra space instead of trying to export when the memory only fits the estimated number.

Practical tip

If output error, read the message and check the capacity before increasing the quality or retrying the entire video.

Frequently asked questions

Shorten or lower bitrate to reduce capacity more?

Both are active. Cut out the part that doesn't need to directly reduce the duration; Lowering the bitrate reduces the amount of data spent per second of audio.
