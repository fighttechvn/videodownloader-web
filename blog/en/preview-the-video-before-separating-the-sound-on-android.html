Preview the video before separating the sound on Android

FightTechSeptember 20, 20262 min read

Videos with similar names can easily be mistakenly selected. Thumbnails and previews help you identify content before creating an additional MP3 file. This is useful when the camera folder contains multiple takes of the same show or meeting.

What you will do

Use the video thumbnail in Video to Audio to preview, identify the correct clip and select the segment to separate before exporting the audio file.

Steps

  1. Open the Convert tab and search for videos by name, thumbnail, and display duration.
  2. Touch the thumbnail area to open the video player, listen to the audio clip and check the content.
  3. Return to the list, touch the conversion button of the video you just checked.
  4. Set the cut interval if necessary, give the result a recognizable name, then export the audio.

Thumbnails and toggle buttons serve two different things

Thumbnail opens the content for viewing; toggle button opens export options. When multiple videos are being selected, touch operations can switch to selecting or deselecting files. If you want to preview a clip, leave multi-selection mode first to avoid mistaken operations.

Practical tip

Compare both the duration and the first sound of the clip; One beautiful frame is not enough to confirm the correct recording.

Frequently asked questions

What if the video is playable but there is no sound?

Check that the video has an actual audio track and has sound within the selected range. Conversion does not recreate audio that is not present in the video.

Before you start

Advanced options, export quality or the number of files in one run may use credits or Premium. Check the details on the action screen before running; the interface can vary slightly by version and language.
