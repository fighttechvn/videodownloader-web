Split an interview into MP3 on your phone

FightTechSeptember 20, 20262 min read

You don't need to export the entire interview just to hear one answer again. The clipping option in the conversion stream helps keep a specific amount of time. A good paragraph should have enough questions or leads so the listener knows what the character is talking about.

What you will do

Select the start and end points to separate an answer in an interview video into MP3 using Video to Audio on Android.

Steps

  1. Watch the video and record the time the question starts and the time the answer ends.
  2. Open the conversion options, select MP3 and set two cut markers around the section to get.
  3. Listen for boundary testing if possible; to add a short space before the first consonant and after the last sentence.
  4. Name your answer by topic, export the file, then listen back to the entire episode before sharing.

Cut it short but keep the speaker's meaning

Cutting too close can cause the opening word to be lost, making the sentence sound different or missing a subject. Keep natural breaks between ideas. If a later paragraph explains or corrects an earlier statement, the full explanation should be included rather than just a sentence that is easily misunderstood.

Practical tip

When you need multiple separate answers, export each paragraph with its own topic name for quick retrieval.

Frequently asked questions

Does cutting a part make the original video shorter?

The audio separation stream creates its own result file. You still use the source video to compare milestones and re-export another segment.
