Control music with widgets on the Android screen

FightTechSeptember 20, 20262 min read

Widget helps you pause a song you're listening to without having to find the application icon again. Video to Audio has two types of music control widgets: Mini and Standard. Choose a small version if your home screen is cramped, or a cover photo version if you want to identify the article more easily.

What you will do

Add Video to Audio Mini or Standard widget to view song titles and control music playback right on the Android home screen.

Steps

  1. Long press an empty area on the Android home screen and open the launcher's Widgets list.
  2. Find Video to Audio and select Mini 3×1 or Standard 4×1 according to available space.
  3. Open a song from the Music tab in the app to start a playback session.
  4. Return to the home screen, check the song title and use the play or pause button on the widget.

Widgets are player controls

The widget reflects the song currently playing in the app, not a standalone music library. When there is no play session, tapping the control can open the Music tab for you to choose a song. How to add and resize widgets depends on the launcher in use, so system menu names may vary between phones.

Practical tip

If you change to a new song when the app is in the background and you don't hear sound, reopen the player and press play to check.

Frequently asked questions

Does the widget automatically convert videos to MP3?

No. You switch and select songs in the app; widget for viewing status and controlling music.
