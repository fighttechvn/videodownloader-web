Why is there no sound when separating audio from video?

FightTechSeptember 20, 20262 min read

When the result is silent, check the source before changing the bitrate. The video may not originally have an audio track, or the selected interval may contain only silent footage. Determining the correct cause helps avoid re-exporting multiple versions with the same problem.

What you will do

Check the audio track, cuts and decoding capabilities when Video to Audio outputs the file silently or the video audio is unreadable.

Steps

  1. Play the source video and increase the media volume just enough to confirm there is actually sound.
  2. Check cut markers: whether the selected section is in the section with lyrics or music.
  3. Try extracting a short clip with clear audio, keeping the output settings simple.
  4. If the error persists, try another video to distinguish the problem of the source file with read permissions or decryption capabilities on the device.

The shell file format doesn't tell the whole story about the sound inside

Two videos with the same MP4 extension may contain different audio codecs. A file that plays in a dedicated app may not be supported in the same way by your phone's codec. When you need to source from another device, choose a popular audio output method and try a short sample first.

Practical tip

Increasing from 128 to 320 kbps did not restore the missing audio track.

Can the app restore the sound of a muted video?
