Separate the lecture audio to listen when the screen is turned off

FightTechSeptember 20, 20262 min read

A lecture video can contain many minutes of just listening to the explanation. When you convert audio to MP3, you have something to review on the go and don't need to keep the video screen on. Choose a paragraph that has enough context to still understand the lesson without looking at the picture.

What you will do

Extract lecture audio from video using Video to Audio, save MP3 and listen to it with the built-in player when the phone screen is turned off.

Steps

  1. Open the Convert tab, find the lecture video, and preview the beginning to confirm the correct lesson.
  2. Touch the conversion button, select MP3; If reviewing only one section, place the start and end points around that explanation.
  3. Give it a name with the subject and session number, for example vat-ly-buoi-03, then start converting.
  4. Open the results in the Music tab, play the song, then turn off the screen; Use notification controls to pause or resume.

Which part should you choose to still understand?

Definitions, summaries, and Q&A sections are often appropriate for audio. The lecture depends on charts or operations on the board, so keep the video for comparison. Listen for a minute before separating the entire course: if you continuously listen to “like this picture”, the audio version may not be enough for independent study.

Practical tip

Keep the original video for exercises with pictures; MP3 version serves to listen to the lecture again.
