Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Video to MP3 ("the app") is an audio converter for Android published by FightTech. This policy explains what the app accesses and how your data is handled. In short: your videos and MP3 files never leave your device, and we do not sell your data.

What the app does

The app lists the videos already stored on your device, and converts the one you pick into an MP3 file. The conversion is performed entirely on your device by a built-in encoder. Your video is never uploaded to a server, and neither is the resulting audio. There is no account and no cloud processing.

Permissions we use

  • Media access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO on Android 13+, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older versions) — to list the videos you can convert, to save the MP3 to your Music library, and to show your converted files in the Music tab.
  • Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used for ads, purchases, analytics and crash reports, the feedback form and the optional online song-info lookup. Converting does not require a connection.
  • Foreground service & media playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) — to keep playback running in the background with controls in your notification shade.

Your files

Converted MP3 files are written to the public Music/Video to Audio folder on your device and registered with your Music library so other apps can see them. Your original video is never modified or deleted. The app keeps no history, no playlists and no account — nothing about your files is transmitted anywhere.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app includes Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google). They collect anonymous, aggregated information such as which features are used, app version, device model, OS version, country derived from IP address, and — when the app crashes — a stack trace and device state. They never receive your videos, your MP3 files or their names, and we do not track which videos you convert. We use this data only to fix bugs and understand which features matter. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

Advertising

The free version is supported by ads served through Google AdMob (Google) — banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads — with Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) as a fallback ad network. To request and measure an ad, these providers receive your device's advertising identifier, IP address and basic device information (model, OS version, country, language, ad interactions). They do not receive your videos or their names. This is a transfer of data to third parties and is declared as such on the app's Google Play Data safety page. A rewarded ad is shown only when you choose to watch it, and earns you 1 credit (up to 5 per day).

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent form before any ad is requested, and your choice is remembered on every later launch. You can opt out of ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Google → Ads, or remove ads entirely with Premium.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how AdMob uses data and the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Our authorized sellers are declared in app-ads.txt.

Feedback you send us

Settings → Send feedback lets you write to us. What you type — the subject, your message, and an optional email address if you want a reply — is sent to our server at fighttech.vn, along with basic technical details about your device (model, Android version, screen size, language, available memory) that help us reproduce the problem. The email address is optional: leave it blank and the report is sent without it. We use what you send only to reply to you and to fix the issue — never for advertising, and we never sell it. Ask us at the address below and we will delete your report. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for how you use the audio you extract. Only convert material you own or have the right to use, and respect the copyright of the content you process.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example if analytics is added in a future version — this page will be updated and the date above revised.
